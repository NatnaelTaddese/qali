## What's new in 0.1.8

- **Notifications on your Mac.** Event reminders and booking requests now arrive as Mac notifications, with the same chime as Qali on the web. While you're using Qali they appear inside the window instead. Choose where they show in Settings → Events → Notifications.
- **Search Settings.** Type in the field at the top of the Settings sidebar to find any setting.
- **A fresh look for Settings.** Each pane has its own coloured icon, and switches, buttons and menus sit centred on their rows.
- **Today shows you where it is.** Clicking Today briefly highlights today's date in the week view and today's cell in the month view.
- **Round close buttons.** Panels in the bottom island close with a round glass button.
