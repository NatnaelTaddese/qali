## What's new in 0.1.13

- **Auto mode for Qali.** Turn on Auto mode from Qali's contact card (click Qali's name at the top of the panel), and Qali makes simple changes to your own events right away, such as adding an event or moving one only you are in. Anything with guests, any repeating event and any cancellation still waits for you to confirm.
- **Clearer change cards.** The details on Qali's change cards are easier to read over every event colour, and a change Qali made itself now says what it did, such as "Rescheduled", instead of "Drafted".
- **Room between back-to-back events.** Events that start as another ends now have a small gap between them, so you can tell where one stops and the next begins.
