## What's new in 0.1.5

- **Booking link.** Scheduling is now in the Mac app. The booking link button in the bottom island shows whether your link is live and lets you switch it on or off. You can copy the link or share it through the system share menu. A badge counts the people waiting on you.
- **Confirm or decline requests.** Pending requests appear as dashed blocks on the calendar and in a list in the island. Each shows the person's message and the time zone they booked in. Confirm adds the event to your calendar and invites them. Booking notifications open the request.
- **Set up your page in Settings.** The new Settings → Booking Link pane holds your link name, meeting title, slot length, notice, how far ahead people can book, and your weekly hours. Changes go live only when you press Save.
- **Paint specific dates.** Choose "Set specific dates" and drag on the calendar to give a single day its own hours. Click a span to remove it, or use "Use Weekly" to put a day back on your usual hours.
- **Date in the menu bar.** The menu bar item's date is now today's day number in Qali's serif, without the calendar icon.
- **Settings sidebar refresh.** The Settings sidebar uses monochrome outline icons with the selected pane in bold. Rows with a description now centre their controls.
- **Fixes.** In the week view, the all-day "+N" toggle stays clickable when an event from an earlier day spans past it.
