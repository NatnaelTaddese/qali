## What's new in 0.1.12

- **Ask Qali.** The assistant is now in the Mac app. Click the ghost in the bottom-right corner or press ⌘J, and ask about your calendar in a conversation that works like Messages.
- **Nothing changes until you say so.** When Qali offers to add, move or cancel an event, it shows a card to confirm or discard, and the calendar jumps to the change once it's made.
- **Never miss a reply.** Close the panel while Qali is answering and a blue dot on the ghost tells you when it's done. Earlier conversations are in the history menu, where you can also delete them.
- **Pick Qali's photo.** Choose Sticker, Grey or Ink under Settings › General › Appearance.
