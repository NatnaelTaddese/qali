## What's new in 0.1.10

- **Set an event in another time zone.** If you keep other time zones, the event form has a zone chip above the start. Pick one of your zones, or any other, and the times read in that zone with yours alongside. The event is saved to Google in that zone.
- **Events remember their time zone.** Editing an event opens it in the zone it was set in, and its details mark that zone.
- **Travelling.** When your Mac changes time zone, Qali offers to show your calendar in the new zone and moves your old one beside your hours. Turn it off in Settings → Date & Time.
- **Add another Google account.** Settings → Accounts now links a second account inside the app, instead of signing you in on the web.
- **Clearer menu bar countdown.** The menu bar says "in 25m" before an event and "25m left" once it has started, next to the event's colour bar.
