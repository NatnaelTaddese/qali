## What's new in 0.1.9

- **Feel events snap into place.** On a Force Touch trackpad, dragging an event taps lightly at each quarter hour and knocks twice when it moves to another day. Turn it off in Settings → General → Haptic feedback.
- **Qali's own notification sounds.** Mac notifications now play Qali's chime for reminders and booking requests, instead of the system's default sound.
- **Tidier event cards.** Events on the week and day grid have the same corners as all-day events, so the colour bar sits neatly inside them.
- **Delete your account.** Settings → Accounts now ends with Delete account, which permanently removes your Qali account and its data.
