## What's new in 0.1.6

- **Other time zones.** Add up to two time zones in Settings → Date & Time. Each gets its own column of hours beside yours in the day and week views, with a three-letter code (TYO, LDN…) and how far it is from you. A sun and a moon mark 6 AM and 6 PM there, a "+1" tag shows where its next day starts, and its current time sits level with yours.
- **Saved to your account.** Your other time zones follow your account, so they're there on every device you sign in on.
- **Zone menu.** Click a zone's code above the hours to make it your time zone (your old one takes its place), move it left or right, remove it, or open Time Zone Settings.
- **Times under the pointer.** Turn on "Show times under the pointer" in Settings → Date & Time to see, as you hover over the hours, that time in each of your zones.
- **Event times in your other zones.** Event details list a timed event's times in each of your zones, with its day there when that differs from yours.
- **World clock in the menu bar.** The menu bar panel shows a clock for your zone and each other one. Settings → Menu Bar → World clock chooses what it shows, and a new "World clock" style puts one zone's time in the menu bar itself.
- **Opens ready.** Qali remembers your settings, so the calendar opens in your time zone and week layout straight away instead of adjusting a moment later.
- **Menu bar panel.** Open Qali, Settings… and Quit now look like standard menu items, and the panel no longer cuts off its date header.
