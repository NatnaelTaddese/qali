## What's new in 0.1.7

- **What's new, once.** After an update, Qali shows what changed the first time it opens, like this.
- **Guests on event cards.** Event cards in the day and week views show their first guests along the bottom when there's room. Guests without a photo get a coloured initial, the same colour everywhere you see them.
- **Settings from your account.** The account panel in the bottom island has a Settings button above Sign out.
- **All-day events stay out of your booking link.** Holidays, trips and other all-day events no longer block the hours on your booking page. To have them block those days again, turn off Settings → Booking Link → Ignore all-day events.
- **Fixes.** "Use Weekly" on a day's header works again while you set specific dates.
